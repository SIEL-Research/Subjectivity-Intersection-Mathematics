# Subjectivity Intersection Mathematics preprint v0.1

This release publishes:

**Subjectivity Intersection Mathematics: A Common Pointed-Braid Source for
Quantum History and Lorentzian Geometry**

The preprint constructs a single typed mathematical system in which a quantum
history and an initial Lorentzian geometry are read from one fixed finite
pointed-braid source and subsequently evolved in one coupled local system with
relational backreaction. It combines exact finite algebraic certificates with
explicit bridge assumptions.

The main theorem is conditional, fixed-finite, local to one regular patch, and
valid on a nonzero short-time interval. This working preprint is not peer
reviewed and does not establish an empirically validated theory of quantum
gravity, absolute semiclassical gravity, or an ontological theory of
subjectivity.

The release contains the exact reviewed v0.1 PDF, the frozen LaTeX source, and
the metadata, provenance, and verification records needed to audit the release.
