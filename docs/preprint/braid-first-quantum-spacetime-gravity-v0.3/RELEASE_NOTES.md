## Preserved boundaries

The manuscript does not claim a completed quantum-gravity theory, a derivation
of general relativity, independent Braid selection of the full six-sector
Riemann tensor, combined stationarity of the two finite actions, a neighborhood
Einstein solution, continuum or low-energy recovery, physical units,
empirical gravity, or a derivation of consciousness. The identification of
Absolute, Relative, and Intersection Subjectivity with light, time/clock, and
space is an ontological interpretation rather than a theorem of the finite
certificates.
