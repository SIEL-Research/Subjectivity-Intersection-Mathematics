Modern physics succeeds by extracting quantities that do not depend on arbitrary coordinates, gauges, or laboratory presentations. The methodological value of objectivity is not in dispute here. The disputed step is ontological: must the invariant structure be there first, independently of every standpoint, with perspectives entering only as partial descriptions of it? Call that stronger commitment \emph{objectivist primacy}. It is stronger than scientific reproducibility, stronger than observer agreement, and stronger than covariance.

The distinction matters because invariance alone does not specify the order of construction. One may begin with an objective object and derive its appearances. Alternatively, one may begin with distinct standpoints and derive a public invariant by quotienting only their representational redundancy. Both routes can end with an invariant. They disagree about what is primitive.

This paper develops the second route as the presently proven core of the Braid-first programme. The mathematical work is motivated by Subjectivity Intersection Mathematics and its pointed-unitary-braid realization \cite{watanabe2026sim,watanabe2026braid}. Its first aim is not to replace the predictions of established physics. It is to establish that the same source can generate classical objective structure and finite gravity dynamics, and that the primacy of objectivity is therefore not forced by the demand for exactness, covariance, reproducibility, or stable shared structure.

The proposal is close enough to relational quantum mechanics, quantum reference frames, QBism, perspectival realism, and structural realism that careful separation is essential. Relational quantum mechanics denies an observer-independent quantum state and treats facts as relational \cite{rovelli1996}. QBism treats the quantum state as an agent's personal probabilistic assignment rather than an objective property \cite{fuchs2013}. Perspectival realism studies reliable scientific knowledge across situated perspectives \cite{massimi2022,massimi2023}. Structural realism and invariantism identify objective content with transformation-invariant structure \cite{weyl1952,ladyman1998}. The present proposal shares relational and perspectival motivations, but makes a different technical claim: an objective tensor carrier is an \emph{output} of the maintained distinction and intersection of two source standpoints, and the same output is regenerated at every finite stage of a nonterminal braid tower.

It names the non-personal relative mode associated, in the proposed physical interpretation, with time, clocking, ordering, and comparison. Human and other biological subjects may instantiate highly organized forms of relative standpoint, but they do not constitute or originate Relative Subjectivity.

This usage is ontological rather than psychological. It says that a physical description is made from somewhere in the structure, and that the difference among such ``somewheres'' can be constitutive rather than merely epistemic. On the proposed ontology, Relative Subjectivity therefore belongs to physical reality prior to life: clock-like relative ordering and standpoint difference do not begin with humanity, and would obtain in a prebiotic universe. This temporal priority is an ontological interpretation of the physical identification, not a theorem of the present Braid certificates. A separate bridge axiom and separate evidence would be required to connect this notion to consciousness.

Under three explicit bridges, the independently fixed source also determines a smooth local Lorentz metric two-jet satisfying $G_{\mu\nu}=(3/5)\Theta_{\mu\nu}$ exactly at its base point. Its existence materially strengthens the Creation Principle's physical content. Current-input quantum kinematics, the declared four-dimensional codomain, fixed-packet evidence, explicit Einstein bridges, and the absence of neighborhood general relativity remain explicit.
