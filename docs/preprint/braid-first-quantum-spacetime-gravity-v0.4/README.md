# Subjectivity-Intersection Braid Quantum Gravity v0.4

This directory contains the public-repository working preprint:

> Satoru Watanabe, *Subjectivity-Intersection Braid Quantum Gravity: A
> Constructive Refutation of Objectivist Primacy and a Conditional Continuum
> Einstein Equation*, v0.4 (19 September 2026).

Version 0.4 unifies the finite Braid construction and its continuum extension
as a bounded quantum-gravity candidate grounded in subjectivity intersection.
The finite source results are exact within the declared model. Under four
explicit bridge laws, NL1--NL4, a continuum metric-affine action yields

```text
G_{mu nu} = (3/5) T_{mu nu}
```

as an Euler--Lagrange equation on a spacetime neighborhood.

The continuum result is conditional. NL1--NL4 have not yet been derived from
the Braid source alone. First-class gravitational closure, quantization of the
continuum gravitational field, ultraviolet completion, and empirical
gravitational validation remain open. “Subjectivity” denotes a standpoint of
access and transport, not consciousness.

The constructive refutation is therefore scoped precisely: the construction
is a counterexample to the universal necessity of objectivist primacy. It is
not an empirical disproof of every realist ontology and is not presented as a
completed theory of quantum gravity.
