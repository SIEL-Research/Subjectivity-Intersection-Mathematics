This closes the registered all-scale channel and quartic-response package; it does not close arbitrary nonlinear composite observables or physical smooth-spacetime UV completion.

It neither predicts $G$ nor measures a Braid gravitational response. Public AWS metadata do not expose enough clock metrology to claim a complete SI uncertainty budget.

The result is not promoted beyond its proof. The finite residual is only bounded by $O(h)$. The parent is not full ten-component nonlinear gravity, the feedback law is not unique over all dynamics, and repeated nonlinear closure is unproved. The UV theorem does not yet cover the full metric Fr\'echet Hessian, arbitrary nonpolynomial sampling, bounded composite stress, the complete $\sigma_6$/parallel operator, or smooth-spacetime renormalization. The finite direct relative coefficient is $1$, not the distinct full-corner routing ratio $3/5$; their physical relation remains open. Fixed-finite constraint routes through BGCE442 are excluded, but the refinement-limit unbounded-derivation route is open. Full calibration awaits provider-clock metrology, and empirical gravity remains untested.

The philosophical conclusion is constructive. Objective Lorentz structure, conserved stress, and a classical gravity limit need not be primitive in every exact model; they can be generated from nonidentical, marked, standpoint-bearing processes and their lawful intersection. Objectivity is retained as stable public invariance. What is rejected is its universal ontological priority. The physical conclusion is correspondingly measured: this is a serious and increasingly complete Braid quantum-gravity candidate, not yet a completed or empirically validated theory.
