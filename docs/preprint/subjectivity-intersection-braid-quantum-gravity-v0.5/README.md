# Subjectivity-Intersection Braid Quantum Gravity v0.5

This directory contains the public-repository working preprint:

> Satoru Watanabe, *Subjectivity-Intersection Braid Quantum Gravity: A
> Constructive Refutation of Objectivist Primacy with a Source-Derived 1+3
> Carrier and a Two-Principle Conditional Einstein Equation*, v0.5
> (19 September 2026).

Version 0.5 removes a primitive numerical or ambient four-dimensional carrier
from the mathematical starting point. The observer quotient
`Q_obs = V4`, together with an order unit, point separation, and its full
intrinsic affine symmetry, forces a target-free `1+3` readout. One explicitly
labelled Observer Uniform Cauchy Closure principle completes that rational
carrier to `W = R^V4`; it does not add a new direction.

The earlier NL1--NL4 packet is dependency-compressed to two remaining
principles:

- **CGR**: causal geometric realization of the completed carrier, source-local
  net, temporal Cartan link, and finite Palatini cells as local continuum
  geometry;
- **MMR**: marked matter realization and routing into one local metric-only
  matter action with the source-selected relative coupling.

Under CGR and MMR, the continuum metric-affine action yields

```text
G_{mu nu} = (3/5) T_{mu nu}
```

as an Euler--Lagrange equation on a spacetime neighborhood. Neither CGR nor
MMR is yet derived in full from the pointed-braid source. Physical event
localization, continuum-field quantization, ultraviolet completion, and
empirical gravitational validation remain open. “Subjectivity” denotes a
standpoint of access and transport, not consciousness.

The constructive refutation is scoped precisely: this construction is a
counterexample to the universal necessity of objectivist primacy. It is not an
empirical disproof of every realist ontology and is not presented as a
completed theory of quantum gravity.
