# Subjectivity-Intersection Mathematics — Experiment 002: A Blinded Test for Detecting Relational Carriers

Dear colleagues,

I have incorporated the feedback received so far and finalised the plan for
Experiment 002 in the public Subjectivity-Intersection Mathematics research
programme.

I will preregister this plan on GitHub and proceed with the experiment.

The purpose of Experiment 002 is to determine whether our method for
identifying the presence or absence of a relational carrier is itself
reliable, using synthetic data for which the correct classification is known.

In this experiment, A and B are two operational units through which
subjectivity functions as viewpoint, projection, measurement, state, or
action. Experiment 002 implements this definition within a synthetic system.

The generated paired-unit time series will belong to three classes.

1. No relational carrier

The observed relationship can be explained by unit-specific memory, a common
cause, instantaneous coupling, a static common object, or an observational
artefact. No shared history-bearing state is required.

2. A shared history-bearing state, but not a complete relational carrier

A compressed shared state exists, but it lacks one or more necessary
properties, such as pair specificity, joint generation by both units, or
feedback into both units.

The recurrent standard full-adder raised by Luke will be treated as a boundary
case in this class. Its carry state retains history and responds to operation
order, but it has no unit or pair identity and does not act back separately
upon both units. It can therefore demonstrate the presence of a shared
history-bearing state without qualifying as a complete relational carrier.

3. A pair-indexed relational carrier

This is a state that can be generated only through contributions from both A
and B, retains their shared history, is specific to the particular pair, and
subsequently affects the state or output of both A and B.

To prevent the classification method from being fitted to a single generating
mechanism, the positive class will contain two structurally different carrier
families.

The first extends the path-ordered carrier established in Experiment 001, in
which different operation orders generate different states, into a noisy
recurrent time series.

The second uses an independently constructed carrier based on noncommuting
two-by-two matrices over GF(3).

The analysis will not receive the true class labels or latent states. The
ground truth will be sealed during generation. After the analytical procedure
and decision criteria have been frozen, the method will be tested on unseen
histories, unseen pairings, unseen operational units, and the second
independently constructed carrier mechanism.

The principal success criteria are:

- balanced accuracy of at least 0.80 at each transfer level involving unseen
  histories, pairings, and operational units;
- recall of at least 0.70 for every class;
- a false Class 2 declaration rate of no more than 0.10 across the
  carrier-absent and incomplete shared-state classes;
- Class 2 recall of at least 0.80 for the first carrier mechanism;
- Class 2 recall of at least 0.70 for the independent second carrier
  mechanism; and
- an unchanged classification in at least 95% of the registered coordinate
  transformations and relabellings.

If Experiment 002 succeeds, it will provide a domain-neutral method for
distinguishing systems with no relational carrier, systems with an incomplete
shared history-bearing state, and systems requiring a complete relational
carrier.

The next stage will apply this method to external test domains. Candidate
operational units include stellar observation-centred viewpoints, EEG
channels or phase-resolved EEG measurement processes, quantum
preparation-and-measurement contexts and their recorded event streams, and
subjectivity agents with explicitly defined observation boundaries,
persistent internal states, and action channels.

For each target, the operational meanings of A and B, the observables, the
interventions, and the competing explanations will be defined separately and
tested through a dedicated preregistered experiment.

I will now preregister this final plan on GitHub and execute Experiment 002.
Once a coherent set of results has been obtained, I will report it through the
public repository.
