# Preregistration: Experiment 007A Phase 1

## Title

Operator-Algebra Definition Audit Before Independent Seven-Dimensional Comparison

## Status

Preregistered source-boundary audit. Not executed at the time of registration.

This audit is result-informed by the public Experiment 007 Phase 2 result and by subsequent comments from Thomas Loker and Marcel Wende. It is not presented as a blind discovery of the D12RG source object. Its purpose is to freeze the corrected mathematical question and determine whether the two source frameworks currently define objects that can be compared without constructing missing semantics after inspection.

## Background

Experiment 007 Phase 1 found no unique source-defined connector from signed FNIZ operation history through DVJ holonomy to D12 deck action. Experiment 007 Phase 2 exhaustively searched the registered 144-rule constructed family and retained two non-equivalent top classes, `(1,5)` and `(1,7)`.

A proposed Experiment 007A initially treated the seven exponents

`{0, +/-1, +/-2, +/-5} mod 12`

as an exactly seven-element structure closed under the inherited C12 composition. That description is inconsistent: `1 + 2 = 3 mod 12`, and 3 is not in the set. Lagrange's theorem also excludes a seven-element subgroup of C12.

The frozen D12RG sources instead describe seven printed regular matrices as a basis of a seven-dimensional unital algebra generated by one order-12 matrix. The seven basis matrices need not be closed as a set; their linear span may be closed because products reduce to linear combinations of the basis.

## Registered questions

1. What exact algebraic object do the seven frozen D12RG operators form?
2. Are the seven printed matrices closed as a set under ordinary matrix multiplication?
3. Is their seven-dimensional linear span closed under matrix multiplication with exact rational structure constants?
4. Do the frozen Subjectivity-Intersection Mathematics Experiments 006, 006R, and 006A already define one finite primitive operator basis, typed product law, inadmissible cases, identity and inverse rules, and canonical comparison rule?
5. Can a hash-separated SIO-to-D12RG algebra comparison proceed without adding a constructed SIO extraction rule?

## D12RG target audit

The registered runner will verify exactly:

- seven printed rational regular matrices;
- basis exponents `(5,1,11,7,10,2,0)`;
- character exponents `(0,1,2,5,7,10,11)`;
- the order-12 generator relation;
- the degree-seven minimal polynomial;
- linear independence of the seven basis matrices;
- exact reduction of all 49 ordered basis products into the seven-dimensional rational span;
- failure or success of closure of the seven basis matrices as a set; and
- the identity and three reciprocal exponent pairs.

Every ordered product and its exact seven rational coefficients will be published.

## SIO definition audit

The frozen 006-series sources must already supply all of the following before an independent algebra comparison can be classified as ready:

1. one finite primitive operator or basis declaration;
2. typed domains and codomains;
3. an executable composition or product rule;
4. explicit inadmissible or undefined compositions;
5. identity and inverse rules; and
6. a canonical operator-table rule.

Operational definitions of relational-component extraction, recurrent transport, cross-pair exchange, nonseparability, and bilateral effects are audited separately. Their existence does not by itself define an operator algebra.

The audit does not choose a primitive list from formation, reversal, exchange, historical action, bilateral effect, or self-re-entry after reading the outcome. If the source does not already define a unique primitive set, that absence is the result.

## Executable independence boundary

The runner will:

1. inventory and hash the SIO-side source definition before importing any D12RG module;
2. record that digest;
3. verify the frozen D12RG checkout only afterward; and
4. load and audit the D12RG object after the SIO digest has been fixed.

This phase cannot hash a derived SIO operator table unless the frozen SIO sources already define one. A later constructed extraction phase must generate, canonicalise, and hash its SIO table before loading the D12RG comparison object.

## Primary classifications

Exactly one primary classification will be emitted.

### `READY_FOR_HASH_SEPARATED_INDEPENDENT_COMPARISON`

The D12RG target is exactly defined and the frozen SIO sources already define a unique complete typed operator algebra suitable for a preregistered comparison.

### `D12_TARGET_DEFINED_SIO_EXTRACTION_UNDERDEFINED`

The D12RG target is exactly defined, but the frozen SIO sources do not yet define the primitive basis, typed product law, undefined cases, or canonicalisation required for an independent comparison.

### `D12RG_TARGET_UNDERDEFINED`

The frozen D12RG source does not uniquely define the target algebraic object required for comparison.

### `PROVENANCE_FAILURE`

At least one registered repository, commit, path, or SHA-256 check fails. Execution stops without a scientific classification.

## Registered downstream response

If the result is `D12_TARGET_DEFINED_SIO_EXTRACTION_UNDERDEFINED`, no seven-dimensional match will be claimed. The next phase must preregister a constructed SIO operator-extraction rule containing:

- source justification for every primitive;
- one-primitive-removed and one-candidate-primitive-added robustness variants;
- typed domains, codomains, products, and invalid cases;
- a frozen witness set for nonidentity action;
- complete routing for every failed conjunction;
- canonicalisation under the four C12 automorphisms;
- retention of both Phase 2 classes unless an independent invariant distinguishes them; and
- generation and hashing of the SIO table before D12RG is loaded.

If the D12RG target is a seven-dimensional algebra rather than a seven-element closed set, the later comparison must concern the full algebraic signature and exact product structure, not merely seven exponent labels.

## Interpretation boundary

This audit determines whether the mathematical objects required for Experiment 007A are already source-defined. It does not establish a seven-dimensional SIO algebra, an SIO-D12RG isomorphism, Class 2 transfer, O3, or an ontological identity.
