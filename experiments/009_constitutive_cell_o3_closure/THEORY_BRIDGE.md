# Experiment 009 theory bridge

## From object-first biology to constitutive life closure

The central discovery is a change of standpoint, not the addition of a new
cellular score.

Object-first biology assumes the cell as an already constituted entity and
then describes the boundary, metabolism, information processing, repair, and
homeostasis that it possesses. Subjectivity Intersection Mathematics reverses
that order. Differentiated cellular perspectives are not yet one living cell
merely because they coexist. Their intersection generates a distributed third
perspective, and its return into their next states continually forms the cell
as one living whole.

Writing boundary, metabolism, and information-repair as `B_t`, `M_t`, and
`I_t`, the operational creation path is

```text
(B_t, M_t, I_t) -> O3_t -> (B_{t+1}, M_{t+1}, I_{t+1}).
```

`O3_t` is not an installed controller or common floor. It names the
non-reducible whole-relation that emerges from the differentiated perspectives
and re-enters them. The cell is the dynamic higher-order closure constituted by
that return.

## Continuity with the atomic turning point

CP-145 stopped looking for an extra atomic O3 scalar and located atomic closure
in the finite-mass electron–nucleus relation whose bound whole updates both
constituents. Experiment 009 makes the corresponding move for the cell.

- Atom: the electron–nucleus relation maintains one bound whole and returns
  into both constituents.
- Cell: the boundary–metabolism–information/repair relation maintains the
  conditions under which the whole can form again and returns into every
  differentiated process.

Life adds a further level: it does not only maintain a closure; it regenerates
the conditions of its own future closure.

## What the registered measurements mean

- Distributed Perron mode: the third perspective is not reducible to one
  module.
- Held-out cross-gain: the whole-relation returns information into every
  differentiated next state.
- Joint erasure: the components alone do not constitute the living whole.
- Time shift: relation content without the required temporal alignment is not
  sufficient.
- Reinjection: recovery can follow restoration of the constitutive relation,
  without adding an O3 substance.
- Severe damage: death is an irreversible loss of the capacity to regenerate
  closure conditions, not the disappearance of every component.
- Nonliving control: activity or autocatalysis alone is not the registered life
  closure.

No one measurement is defined as life. The conjunction tests whether the
distributed third perspective emerges, self-reenters, and is required for
continued regeneration of the whole.

## Ontological boundary

Experiment 009 is a finite operational realization of `AB -> C -> AB`, not the
ontology prototype itself. The numerical cross-mode does not become Absolute
Subjectivity or exhaust Intersection Subjectivity. The computation tests the
new constitutive standpoint while leaving that distinction intact.
