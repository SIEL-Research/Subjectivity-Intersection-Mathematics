It publicly confirms, in the frozen reduced dynamics, a reversal in the
explanatory order of life.

The model does not assume a completed cell and then measure integration inside

Within this operational realization:

> The cell is not the prior container of boundary, metabolism, and
> information-repair. The cell is the higher-order dynamic closure constituted
> when their differentiated intersection generates a third perspective whose
> self-reentry remakes both the processes and the conditions of their continued
> intersection.

## Self-reentry into every differentiated process

On the publicly frozen held-out lineages, adding the other modules to each
module's self-only predictor improved prediction of that module's next change
by:

- boundary: `+0.18592830169643793 R²`;
- metabolism: `+0.9589473110234781 R²`;
- information-repair: `+0.764230450690136 R²`.

The minimum native gain was `0.18592830169643793`. The corresponding minimum
gains were `0.018647868801700884` after selective joint erasure and
`0.03160361482980101` under the ten-minute time shift. The native minimum was
therefore more than twice the larger registered relation control. Coordinate
rescaling changed the gains by at most `2.220446049250313e-16`.

The result operationally confirms self-reentry: the relation generated by the
differentiated processes carries held-out information back into the next state
of every process.

## Dynamic closure, restoration, and death

For 32 held-out lineages per condition:

- native moderate damage: `32/32` recovered, with median one division;
- selective joint erasure: `0/32` survived;
- ten-minute relational time shift: `0/32` survived;
- timely relation reinjection at minute 50: `32/32` recovered;
- native severe damage: `0/32` survived;
- nonliving matched control: `0/32` survived, with median zero divisions;
- undamaged native control: `32/32` survived, with median one division.

The contrast is constitutive rather than merely quantitative. Joint erasure
and time shift retain the model components while destroying the relation or
its required temporal order. Relation reinjection restores recovery without
adding an O3 substance. Severe damage marks the boundary beyond which the
relation can no longer remake its own continuation conditions. The nonliving
control shows that generic activity alone does not satisfy the registered life
closure.
