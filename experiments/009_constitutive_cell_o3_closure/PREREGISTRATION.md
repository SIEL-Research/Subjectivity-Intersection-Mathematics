# Preregistration: Experiment 009

## Title

Constitutive Cell Closure: Natural Emergence and Self-Reentry of a Distributed
Third Perspective in JCVI-syn3A-Anchored Dynamics

## Status

PREREGISTERED — NOT EXECUTED ON THE E009 CONFIRMATION SEEDS

This status becomes effective only after the registration commit, tag and
GitHub Release `e009-preregistration-v1.0.0`, and its preregistration DOI are
public and verified. Source-hash validation and unit tests may run before that
event. The frozen E009 training and held-out seed ranges may not run before it.

## 1. Constitutive question

Can differentiated boundary, metabolic, and information-repair processes form
one operational living cell by naturally generating a distributed third
perspective whose self-reentry remakes every process and the conditions of
their continued intersection?

This is not the weaker question of whether an already constituted cell has a
high integration score. The registered proposition reverses the explanatory
order: the timely reciprocal relation is tested as constitutive of the cell as
one living whole.

## 2. Result-informed design disclosure

Experiment 009 is a prospective public confirmation derived from private
Creation-Principle experiments LIFE-001 through LIFE-004. LIFE-004 used the
same equations, interventions, thresholds, and fourteen-gate decision rule and
returned a positive result on its own disclosed confirmation seeds. Therefore
E009 is not blind to the model class or expected qualitative outcome.

To create a new prospective test without hiding that history, E009 freezes new
seed ranges that are disjoint from all LIFE-004 development and confirmation
ranges:

- training: `2026090900` through `2026090915` inclusive;
- held-out confirmation: `2026091900` through `2026091931` inclusive.

These seeds have not been executed during E009 construction. A positive result
tests stochastic generalization of the frozen constitutive realization; it is
not independent biological replication.

The reduced dynamics are anchored to the official

## 4. Installed state and prohibited construction

The only installed dynamic states are:

1. boundary integrity;
2. internal nutrient pool;
3. ATP pool;
4. gene-expression capacity;
5. repair capacity;
6. damage load; and
7. biomass.

No O3, third-perspective, closure, life, death, viability, fitness, or global
objective variable is installed. No optimizer maximizes survival, recovery,
division, or biomass. The frozen laws do contain constructed reciprocal local
dependencies, as disclosed by the source.

## 5. Operational third perspective

Three module observables are computed only after simulation:

1. boundary competence;
2. metabolic competence from nutrient and ATP; and
3. information-repair competence from expression and repair.

A one-minute lagged linear predictor is fitted on the frozen training lineages.
The absolute off-diagonal cross-influence matrix defines a Perron mode. It is a
distributed O3 candidate only when every loading is at least `0.25` and its
participation ratio is at least `1.50`.

The Perron mode alone is not defined as life or ontological subjectivity. It is
one registered measurement of the distributed third perspective within the
full intervention pattern below.

## 6. Operational self-reentry

For every module, compare its self-only lagged predictor with the full predictor
including the other two modules. On held-out lineages, the full predictor must
improve prediction of that module's next change by at least `0.10 R²`.

The minimum native gain must also be at least twice the larger minimum gain in
the selective-erasure and ten-minute time-shift controls. Fixed coordinate
rescaling by `[7.0, 0.2, 3.0]` must change no component gain by more than
`1e-10`.

## 7. Frozen constitutive interventions

- moderate damage: amplitude `0.60`, minutes 40–43;
- severe damage: amplitude `1.20`, minutes 40–43;
- joint erasure: after minute 40 remove only the bilinear inclusion-exclusion
  interaction while preserving reference and marginal terms;
- time shift: after minute 40 supply reciprocally coupled values from ten
  minutes earlier;
- relation reinjection: temporarily erase the relation, then restore native
  relations at minute 50 without adding a new state or substance;
- nonliving control: remove repair and division and reduce construction fluxes
  without adding a life label;
- undamaged native control: preserve normal relations without damage.

The key constitutive contrasts retain the same components while changing their
relation or temporal alignment.

## 8. Frozen decision gates

All fourteen gates must pass for
`REDUCED_MODEL_DYNAMIC_O3_SELF_REENTRY_CLOSURE_SUPPORTED`:

1. no O3 state is installed;
2. no optimization objective is used;
3. at least 90% of native held-out lineages recover from moderate damage;
4. median native divisions after recovery is at least one;
5. at most 10% survive selective joint erasure;
6. at most 10% survive time-shifted relations;
7. at least 90% recover after timely relation reinjection;
8. at most 10% survive severe damage;
9. every emergent-mode loading is at least `0.25`;
10. mode participation ratio is at least `1.50`;
11. minimum held-out component cross-gain is at least `0.10`;
12. native minimum cross-gain is at least twice the largest relation-control
    value;
13. fixed coordinate rescaling changes no cross-gain by more than `1e-10`; and
14. the nonliving control has at most 10% survival and zero median divisions.

Failure of any gate yields
`REDUCED_MODEL_DYNAMIC_O3_SELF_REENTRY_CLOSURE_NOT_SUPPORTED`. No gate may be
removed, reinterpreted, or replaced after execution. All 224 held-out lineage
rows and every computed diagnostic must be retained regardless of outcome.

## 9. Interpretation matrix

- All gates pass: the frozen reduced model prospectively supports the
  constitutive realization—distributed third-perspective emergence,
  self-reentry, relation-dependent recovery, and a life/death boundary.
- Mode or prediction gates fail: no registered natural-emergence/self-reentry
  confirmation, even if recovery occurs.
- Intervention gates fail: no registered constitutive-closure confirmation,
  even if a distributed mode is detected.
- Native recovery or reproduction fails: the frozen realization does not
  maintain the registered life closure on E009 seeds.
- Provenance fails: scientific classification stops and is reported as a
  provenance failure rather than repaired post hoc.

## 11. Claim boundary

The novelty tested here is the constitutive standpoint: the cell is the
higher-order closure generated when differentiated cellular perspectives form
a third perspective that returns into and remakes their continuation
conditions. The tests are operational evidence for that standpoint, not its
replacement by an integration metric.

A positive outcome establishes this prospective public realization in the
frozen reduced model. It does not establish the same O3 in the full hybrid
CME–ODE implementation, in living JCVI-syn3A cells, or the ontology by
computation alone.
