# Experiment 007B Feedback Resolution

## Independent SIO construction

The operative experiment uses only the frozen 006, 006R, and 006A sources. D12RG is not loaded and no D12RG dimension, generator, equation count, or normalized-algebra requirement enters the 007B classification.

## Primitive-count anchoring

An earlier planning draft listed seven semantic action families. Thomas Loker identified that this could anchor the construction to a known downstream comparison. Marcel Wende clarified that semantic families and mathematical primitives are not equivalent.

Resolution: the revision is preserved in the audit record, while the executable inventory is generated from exact source-supported typed operation sites without a target cardinality.

## Scalars, types, and closure

Marcel Wende requested explicit scalar domains, typed domains and codomains, a non-finite-closure route, and separate handling of nonlinear structures.

Resolution: the executable carrier is declared over the real numbers. The runner distinguishes linear, affine, nonlinear, and discrete-routing actions; it computes a multiplication table only if every primitive is a linear endomorphism of one common carrier.

## Generator and marking

No canonical generator, orientation, or involution is imposed on the SIO side. Their absence is a result. Any marked D12RG comparison is deferred to Experiment 007C.

## DJS hypergroup proposal

Luke Leighton proposed a Dunkl-Jewett-Spector hypergroup as the generic comparison floor. Thomas Loker requested that the exact hypergroup notion and its minimality be fixed. Marcel Wende noted that DJS already requires an associative probability-measure convolution, identity, involution, support conditions, and continuity conditions.

Resolution: DJS is preregistered as a comparator. It becomes the minimal null only if every required axiom is forced by the frozen SIO sources without additional modelling choices.

## CDC 6600 suggestion

Luke Leighton identified the dual-triple-adder CDC 6600 construction as a possible clue.

Resolution: it is not imported into the 007B base set because it is not an executed relational-state transformation in the frozen 006-series runners. It may be tested in a separately registered extension.

## Publication boundary

Experiment 007B constructs and classifies the SIO-side action object. Experiment 007C will separately define the D12RG target and the evidential standard for any comparison.
