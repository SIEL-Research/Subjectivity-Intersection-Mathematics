# Experiment 008B

## PySCF Lithium Spin-Representation Prediction

E008B freezes a target-free prediction for the ratio of the ground-state
zero-field hyperfine intervals of lithium-6 and lithium-7:

`nu(6Li)/nu(7Li) = 0.28399324742025683`.

The prediction combines three independently frozen pieces:

1. the common Li `2S` electronic contact coefficient calculated by PySCF;
2. the isotope nuclear `g` factors obtained from independently measured
   magnetic moments; and
3. the interval factors generated by the two diagonal-rotation
   representations, `3/2` for lithium-6 and `2` for lithium-7.

The raw isotope ratio is the primary endpoint. PySCF absolute interval
predictions are mandatory secondary diagnostics. The package contains no
target hyperfine frequency.
