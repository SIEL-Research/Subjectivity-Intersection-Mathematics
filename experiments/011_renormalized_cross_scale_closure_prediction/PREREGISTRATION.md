Prospective Renormalized Cross-Scale Closure Prediction

## 1. Primary question

Does the effective third-term mediator threshold discovered in private
atomic–molecular–cellular integration exploration prospectively predict the
raw survival thresholds of three unexecuted bridge representations?

## 2. Exploration disclosure

Private `INT-001` through `INT-003` exploration compared the operational
third-term topology across atom, molecule, and cell; constructed a frozen
reduced mediation model; and evaluated bridge exponents `q=0.5`, `q=1`, and
`q=2`. The exploratory raw thresholds moved, while the transformed mediator
thresholds clustered at `0.504`, `0.506944`, and `0.507422576896`.

E011 is therefore result-informed. It is not a blind derivation. The frozen
reference is `m_90=0.506944`, and the registered E011 bridge exponents, seeds,
and outcomes have not been executed while this package was prepared.

## 3. Frozen transformation and predictions

The lower relational coupling is `lambda`. Its normalized lower-scale
integrity is `lambda^2`. A bridge representation with exponent `q` enters the
cellular process rates through

```text
m = lambda^(2q).
```

The held-out targets and prospective raw 90% survival thresholds are:

| q | Predicted lambda_90 | Predicted m_90 |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.75 | 0.6357797016112381 | 0.506944 |
| 1.25 | 0.7620509255820336 | 0.506944 |
| 1.50 | 0.7973579507418472 | 0.506944 |

These predictions are fixed as `lambda_90=m_90^(1/(2q))` before target
execution.

## 4. Frozen targets

Each family uses 96 new stochastic lineages at every coupling from `0.400` to
`0.920` in steps of `0.002`. The registered ensemble seeds are `2026130100`,
`2026130200`, and `2026130300`. All other cellular dynamics, the moderate
damage pulse, the intervention interval, recovery criteria, and death criteria
are frozen from the public E009 reduced dynamics.

## 5. Frozen decision gates

The positive decision requires all seven gates:

1. all three `lambda_90` thresholds are observed;
2. every raw `lambda_90` is within `0.010` of its prediction;
3. every transformed `m_90` is within `0.012` of `0.506944`;
4. the held-out `m_90` range is at most `0.020`;
5. the raw `lambda_90` range is at least `0.100`;
6. the transformed survival curves have pooled RMSE at most `0.050`; and
7. the raw thresholds preserve the registered order for increasing `q`.

All gates yield
`RENORMALIZED_CROSS_SCALE_CLOSURE_PREDICTION_SUPPORTED`. If the two mediator
threshold gates pass but another gate fails, the decision is
`RENORMALIZED_CROSS_SCALE_CLOSURE_PREDICTION_PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`. Otherwise
the decision is `RENORMALIZED_CROSS_SCALE_CLOSURE_PREDICTION_NOT_SUPPORTED`.
Every outcome and curve will be retained and released.

## 6. Interpretation

A positive result would prospectively support representation-covariant
closure in the frozen reduced bridge: raw thresholds change with the bridge
coordinate, while the effective mediator relation predicts their transformed
location.

It would not establish a universal numerical constant, GMR, pentagonal
geometry, an ab initio atom-to-cell causal chain, a new physical force, or
empirical biological confirmation. The bridge exponent is an operational
representation parameter, and the cellular dynamics remain a reduced model.
