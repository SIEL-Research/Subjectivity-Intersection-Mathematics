- triton-electron mass ratio: `5496.92153551`;
- helion-electron mass ratio: `5495.88527984`.

Source: NIST, *2022 CODATA adjustment — Fundamental Physical Constants,
Extensive Listing*, https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/pdf/all.pdf

The molecular geometry line (`cc-pVTZ`, `b = 1.0 Å`, `a = 1.3...2.5 Å`) and
all molecular trajectory seeds were selected after E013 exploration but were
not executed there. The cellular calibration and held-out cohort seeds are
outside the E009, E011, E012, and E013-exploration ranges recorded in this
repository.
