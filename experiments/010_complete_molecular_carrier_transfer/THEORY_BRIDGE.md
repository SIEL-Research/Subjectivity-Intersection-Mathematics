## Subjectivity-Intersection connection

The four atomic centres are differentiated perspectives only in the formal,
operational sense that each defines a physical local subspace. Their
intersection is the molecular relation not available to any isolated centre.
Its return is measured by changes to every local population and to the joint
geometry.

The registered viewpoint reverses object-first explanation. It does not begin
with an `H4+` molecule and attach a closure score. It begins with matched
isolated atomic subspaces and asks whether the full relation is constitutive of
the molecular object.

spanned by conventional Hamiltonian and density features. E010 therefore does

predict the target numbers. Its novelty claim is limited to the constitutive
