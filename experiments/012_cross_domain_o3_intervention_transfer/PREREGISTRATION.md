# Experiment 012 preregistration

## Question

Do independently executed held-out atomic, molecular, and cellular systems
share one O3 causal intervention topology without receiving a common encoded
bridge law?

## Confirmatory targets

- Atomic: open soft-Coulomb relaxation at the hydrogen and deuterium reduced
  masses, neither executed in the E012 exploration.
- Molecular: four preregistered stochastic trajectories per E010 basis family
  on the independently computed `H4+` FCI surfaces. These stochastic seeds and
  schedules were not executed during exploration.
- Cellular: four new 16-lineage cohorts under the E009 native, joint-erased,
  time-shifted, and reinjected interventions. These seeds are disjoint from
  E009, E011, and local E012 exploration.

## Frozen causal signature

For every held-out realization:

1. intact score is at least `0.80`;
2. removal score is at most `0.50`;
3. mismatched-return score is at most `0.50`;
4. correct-return score is at least `0.80`;
5. correct return exceeds both removal controls by at least `0.25`.

Across all held-out realizations, the minimum intact-or-correct score must also
strictly exceed the maximum removed-or-mismatched score. This threshold-free
gate prevents confirmation from depending only on the chosen `0.80/0.50`
cutoffs.

## Cross-domain decision

`CROSS_DOMAIN_O3_INTERVENTION_TRANSFER_SUPPORTED` requires:

- every realization in all three domains passes all five gates;
- the threshold-free cross-domain separation is positive;
- the three engines receive no shared scalar mediator; and
- each domain passes when treated as the third held-out domain relative to the
  signature fixed by the other two.

Every other outcome is retained as mixed or unsupported.

## Epistemic boundary

A positive result supports a shared causal intervention topology across three
independently executed reduced domain models. It does not establish a common
O3 substance, a microscopic atom-to-cell law, or laboratory confirmation in
living cells. The molecular trajectories reuse previously computed E010
surfaces but introduce new frozen stochastic dynamics; they are not a new
ab-initio molecule calculation.
