# Experiment 012 interpretation

The preregistered overall decision is:

`CROSS_DOMAIN_O3_INTERVENTION_TRANSFER_NOT_SUPPORTED`

The result is mixed rather than null across every test.

- Atomic transfer passed all gates for hydrogen and deuterium.
- Cellular transfer passed all gates in four previously unused seed cohorts.
- Molecular transfer passed all gates in cc-pVDZ, but failed the registered mismatched-return gate in STO-3G and 6-31G. STO-3G also failed the registered specific-return advantage.
- Removal reduced the whole-formation score in every realization, and correct return restored a high score in every realization.
- The threshold-free separation margin remained positive (`0.207458764`), but the preregistered decision required every domain and every realization to pass every applicable gate.

Accordingly, Experiment 012 does not support the strong registered claim that the complete four-condition O3 intervention signature transfers uniformly across all three independently executed reduced domain models and all held-out realizations. It does support a narrower recorded pattern: O3 removal and correct return were directionally consistent across all nine realizations, while mismatch specificity was representation-sensitive in the molecular engine.

No shared scalar mediator entered the atomic, molecular, or cellular dynamics. The result does not establish a common O3 substance, a microscopic atom-to-cell law, or laboratory confirmation in living cells.
