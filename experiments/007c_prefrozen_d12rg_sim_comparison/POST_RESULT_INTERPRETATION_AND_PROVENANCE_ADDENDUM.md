# Experiment 007C Post-result Interpretation and Provenance Addendum

Date: 2026-08-12

Status: PUBLIC CORRECTION / ORIGINAL PREREGISTRATION AND OUTPUTS PRESERVED

## Purpose

This addendum corrects the provenance designation and interpretive scope of Experiment 007C after review of the complete 007A--007C record and subsequent feedback from Marcel Wende and Luke Leighton.

The preregistration, registered runner, executed outputs, hashes, commits, and primary classification are not altered.

## Correction 1: provenance designation

Experiment 007A froze, before Experiment 007B, a D12RG source object consisting of the seven-dimensional unital operator algebra, its registered basis, order-12 generator, exact rational-span product closure, source commit, and source hashes.

That fact is distinct from freezing the complete Experiment 007C comparison designation before the Experiment 007B result. The later 007C comparison contract selected the specific `4+2+1` projector structure and marked normalization/reconstruction readout, and fixed their use after the 007B action structure was known.

Experiment 007C should therefore be described as:

`SOURCE_CONSTRAINED_PREREGISTERED_POST_RESULT_STRUCTURAL_COMPARISON`

It should not be described as a blind convergence test or as a completely pre-frozen-target comparison.

## Correction 2: target layer

The Paper 5.3 seven-dimensional operator algebra compared in Experiment 007C is a downstream D12RG readout. It is not the primitive scalar operator structure identified by Luke Leighton as the intended comparison source.

That proposed primitive layer acts on scalar roots of unity and is described in terms of operations including involution, defect, Kronecker delta, correction, closure at the limit, and identity, with a closed normalized hypergroup structure. D12RG and its multidimensional operator representations are downstream readouts of that proposed scalar layer.

Experiment 007C did not define or test this scalar hypergroup.

## Result that remains valid

The registered Experiment 007C result remains:

`NO_REGISTERED_CORRESPONDENCE`

Specifically, no map satisfied the registered exact correspondence rules between:

- the Experiment 007B eleven-primitive typed nonlinear SIM action structure; and
- the Paper 5.3 seven-dimensional rational operator-algebra readout selected by the 007C contract.

The provenance checks for those executed inputs passed. The count-only control also remains valid: 330 unmarked seven-subsets exist among eleven SIM primitives, while none survives the complete registered type, composition, generator, involution, and readout constraints.

## Claims withdrawn or narrowed

Experiment 007C does not establish that no correspondence exists between SIM and:

- the proposed primitive scalar root-of-unity structure;
- the proposed closed normalized hypergroup;
- D12RG in general; or
- another independently frozen source-derived representation.

The phrase "direct D12RG--SIM correspondence not supported" must be read narrowly as referring only to the two executed operational objects and the registered 007C mapping contract.

## Next research gate

No revised threshold or retrospective remapping will be applied to Experiment 007C.

A later experiment must first audit the D12RG-side primitive scalar structure without loading SIM materials. It must freeze the scalar carrier, roots of unity, complete operation set, typed laws, product or convolution, identity, involution, normalization, defect, correction, Kronecker-delta role, limit closure, and the map to downstream readouts.

Only after that source object is uniquely defined may an independently selected SIM-side candidate be exposed to it in a new preregistered comparison.
