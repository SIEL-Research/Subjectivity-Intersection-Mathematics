# Preregistration: Experiment 007C

## Title

Pre-frozen, Hash-separated Comparison of the D12RG Paper 5.3 `4+2+1` Algebra and the Subjectivity-Intersection Mathematics Action Structure

## Status

Preregistered and not executed at the time of registration.

This is a source-constrained confirmatory comparison, not a blind discovery study. The D12RG target was frozen by Experiment 007A before the independent SIM extraction in Experiment 007B. The comparison contract, classifications, controls, and failure routing are fixed before execution.

## Correction of the comparison target

The D12RG materials distinguish several mathematically different objects carrying seven labels. Experiment 007C uses only the Paper 5.3 seven-dimensional rational operator algebra with its intrinsic cyclotomic decomposition `4+2+1`.

The following are explicitly excluded:

- the distinct `3+3+1` decomposition;
- Paper 5.3 `E7`;
- Paper 5 `X7`;
- the seven-plus-one extensions;
- any seven-labelled structure introduced after the Experiment 007A freeze; and
- any target selected because it resembles the Experiment 007B result.

The target is therefore correctly described as pre-frozen and hash-separated. It is not a post-result target selection, but neither is it a blind discovery because the D12RG object was known when the comparison programme was designed.

## Frozen objects

### D12RG object

Experiment 007A froze the Paper 5.3 object as:

- a seven-dimensional unital rational operator algebra;
- basis exponents `(5,1,11,7,10,2,0)`;
- character exponents `(0,1,2,5,7,10,11)`;
- one generator `C` of exact order 12;
- minimal polynomial `Phi1 * Phi6 * Phi12`;
- reciprocal involution `C^a -> C^(-a)`;
- intrinsic ranks `4+2+1` selected by the cyclotomic projectors;
- complete exact reduction of all 49 ordered basis products; and
- marked admissible readout identities `R N = I` and `N R = Pi_adm`.

The exact repository, commit, paths, and SHA-256 values are frozen in `registration_manifest.json`.

### SIM object

Experiment 007B independently froze:

- 16 source-supported operation sites;
- 11 canonical primitives;
- 12 explicit data types;
- a complete typed-composability table;
- linear, nonlinear, and discrete-routing actions;
- a mediation route from state bundle through relational extraction, intervention, recurrent advance, and renewed relation;
- no common linear-endomorphism carrier;
- no source-defined global identity, generator, or involution; and
- a stable primary classification under all registered robustness variants.

No primitive is added, removed, renamed, or redefined in Experiment 007C.

## Primary question

Does a structure-preserving correspondence exist between the frozen Experiment 007B SIM action structure and the pre-frozen Experiment 007A D12RG Paper 5.3 `4+2+1` algebra when types, directions, composition, generator, involution, projectors, normalization, reconstruction, and readout markings are all retained?

## Mechanical map generation

Candidate maps are generated only from the signatures in `comparison_contract.json`.

A SIM primitive can realize a D12RG marked role only if all registered constraints for that role hold, including arity, input and output types, linearity class, parameter freedom, direction, and exact algebraic identities. Prose labels and semantic similarity are ignored.

The runner enumerates every admissible role assignment generated by these rules. Manual selection, post-result relabeling, and partial name matching are prohibited.

## Registered comparison levels

### Level 1: complete algebra isomorphism

This requires a bijective structure-preserving map on a common scalar carrier that preserves total multiplication, identity, associativity, commutativity, the order-12 generator, and reciprocal involution.

### Level 2: marked typed-readout correspondence

This requires type- and direction-preserving realizations of `Pi_adm`, `N`, and `R`, together with exact counterparts of `R N = I` and `N R = Pi_adm`. The registered SIM mediation and self-re-entry route must commute with the mapped D12RG readout route.

### Level 3: generic involution or invariant-readout correspondence

This requires at minimum a source-defined global involution on the compared SIM carrier or a source-defined invariant scalar/row preserved by every compared action. A local swap, donor permutation, or counterfactual subtraction is not sufficient.

## Primary classifications

Exactly one classification will be emitted:

- `COMPLETE_MARKED_READOUT_CORRESPONDENCE`
- `PARTIAL_REGISTERED_CORRESPONDENCE`
- `GENERIC_INVOLUTION_OR_TRACE_ONLY_CORRESPONDENCE`
- `NO_REGISTERED_CORRESPONDENCE`
- `PROVENANCE_FAILURE`

`COMPLETE_MARKED_READOUT_CORRESPONDENCE` requires Levels 1 and 2 in full.

`PARTIAL_REGISTERED_CORRESPONDENCE` requires a complete Level 2 marked-readout correspondence while Level 1 fails, or a complete algebraic subcorrespondence explicitly allowed by the contract. Isolated analogies do not qualify.

`GENERIC_INVOLUTION_OR_TRACE_ONLY_CORRESPONDENCE` requires Level 3 while Levels 1 and 2 fail.

`NO_REGISTERED_CORRESPONDENCE` is emitted when none of the three registered levels is satisfied.

`PROVENANCE_FAILURE` terminates the scientific comparison without another classification.

## Controls

The runner reports, without changing the primary classification:

1. count/dimension-only matching;
2. generic involution-only matching;
3. arbitrary common state-label permutations;
4. direction-dropping matching;
5. unmarked abstract matching; and
6. removal of D12RG generator, involution, projector, normalization, and reconstruction markings.

These controls quantify how easily a correspondence could be manufactured by deleting registered structure. A control match is not evidence for the primary claim.

## Secondary Wall-hypergroup boundary

The Wall hypergroup/fusion proposal is recorded only as a secondary comparator because it was proposed after the Experiment 007B result. It cannot alter the target, mapping rules, primary classification, or interpretation of Experiment 007C.

## Interpretation

A positive result would establish only a formal correspondence between the two frozen operational structures at the registered level. It would not establish the Riemann hypothesis, phenomenal subjectivity, ontological identity, or a physical implementation.

A null result would establish that the pre-frozen D12RG marked algebra and the independently extracted SIM action structure do not correspond under the registered exact rules. It would not show that the broader research programmes are unrelated, nor would it license a different D12RG seven or a modified SIM object after inspection.
