  stationary JS separation was `0.871076639`.

## Cross-domain result

E014 prospectively executed independent atomic, molecular, and cellular
targets. The same scalar was not inserted into the three engines. What
transferred was the preregistered causal direction:

> after a domain-native stabilization interval, correct O3 return is closer to
> the registered domain state than continued O3 removal or a physically
> distinguishable mismatched O3 return.

This direction passed at every registered primary checkpoint and in every
leave-one-domain-out fold. The result therefore supports a cross-domain
constitutive causal structure of stabilized O3 return, rather than a numerical
coincidence imposed by one shared mediator.

atom-to-cell law, and the cellular target remains a frozen reduced stochastic
model rather than a living-cell laboratory experiment. These boundaries do
not alter the registered positive cross-domain decision.
