# Experiment 014 preregistration

## Question

Does the stabilized causal advantage of correctly returned O3 transfer
prospectively across fresh atomic, molecular, and cellular reduced
realizations, when every domain retains its own generator, dynamics, state
space, and readout and no shared scalar enters the engines?

## Frozen cross-domain prediction

After a domain-native stabilization interval, correct O3 return must be closer
to the registered domain state than both continued O3 removal and a
physically distinguishable mismatched O3 return.

The transferred object is the causal direction, not a common score, source,
substance, or microscopic law.

## Fresh targets

- Atomic: hydrogenic helium-4 in the frozen soft-Coulomb bridge, using the NIST
  2022 CODATA alpha-particle-to-electron mass ratio.
- Molecular: an unexecuted `H4+` cc-pVTZ geometry line at `b = 1.1 Å`.
- Cellular: unexecuted damage amplitudes `0.675` and `0.825` on four new
  32-lineage cohorts in the frozen E009 reduced stochastic model.

## Primary gates

1. Every stabilized atomic checkpoint has positive correct-minus-removed and
   correct-minus-mismatch Hellinger margins.
2. Every molecular checkpoint has both positive margins at grid factors 1, 2,
   and 4 under exact finite-state propagation and factor-squared time scaling.
3. At both held-out cellular damage amplitudes, reinjection at 45 minutes has
   alive fraction at least 0.95 and reinjection at 50 minutes has alive
   fraction at most 0.05 in every cohort; removal and mismatch remain at most
   0.05.
4. Every cellular checkpoint from 40 minutes after the successful reinjection
   has a one-sided 99% paired bootstrap lower bound above zero against both
   removal and mismatch.
5. Candidate reconstruction and pre-outcome mismatch gates pass.
6. All three leave-one-domain-out directional checks pass.

All gates are conjunctive. One failure yields
`STABILIZED_O3_RETURN_CROSS_DOMAIN_TRANSFER_NOT_SUPPORTED`.

## Mismatch rule

The atomic mismatch is a fixed nonperiodic displacement of the attractive
centre. The molecular runner evaluates only the registered nonperiodic
translations, coordinate warps, and reflection. Before intervention outcomes
are computed it retains structurally distinct alternatives with centered
overlap at most 0.25 and stationary JS separation at least 0.000625, then
selects the least separated eligible alternative. The cellular mismatch is
the frozen E009 time-shifted return. No mismatch is selected using survival,
recovery, or primary target scores.

## Evidential boundary

A positive result supports prospective cross-domain transfer of stabilized
correct-O3 causal specificity across three independent reduced realizations.
It does not establish identical O3 entities, a common physical substance, a
microscopic atom-to-cell law, phenomenal subjectivity, laboratory confirmation
in living cells, or universal instantaneous recovery-speed dominance.
