alpha-particle-to-electron mass ratio in the NIST 2022 CODATA recommended
values:

https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/pdf/JPCRD2022CODATA.pdf

The molecular line (`cc-pVTZ`, `b = 1.1 Å`, `a = 1.3...2.5 Å`) was not
calculated in the E014 exploration. The cellular amplitudes `0.675` and
`0.825` were explicitly reserved and not executed. Their four confirmatory
cohort ranges begin at `2026191001` and do not overlap the exploratory cohort
ranges.

The target choices were informed by E014 local exploration. That exploration
is preserved separately and is not counted as confirmatory evidence.
