# Experiment E015R Preregistration v1.0.0 — Endogenous Standpoint Constitution and Relational-C Re-entry

**PREREGISTERED — NOT EXECUTED**

This Release preregisters a prospective public confirmatory test of whether
action-consequence history can constitute operational self/other standpoint
roles and whether those roles organize a prediction-trained system into a
nonadditive relational component `C` that is causally load-bearing for both
viewpoints and re-enters the next recurrent transition.

The frozen design contains:

- a two-actor, 96-step synthetic interaction with hidden sign-delay causal maps,
  per-step event permutation, reciprocal interaction, and mid-episode map swaps;
- connected and additive 48-dimensional predictors trained on the same
  next-consequence task;
- a four-history inclusion-exclusion definition of `C`;
- 48 fresh outer seeds, with separate debug and randomization seeds;
- nine jointly required primary gates and seven prerequisite validity gates;
- fixed sign-flip, Holm, and equivalence procedures; and
- nonreplacement, retention, stopping, and claim-boundary rules.

E015 is disclosed in the package as the result-informed predecessor. The E015R
confirmatory seeds have not been generated or inspected, and no registered
threshold or analysis may be changed after outcome access.

Confirmatory execution is prohibited until the connected Zenodo integration
publishes DOI-1 and the public Release, DOI record, files, commit, tag, and
checksums are independently reopened and verified in
`registration_receipt.json`.

Every E015R outcome will be retained and published under the distinct result
tag `e015r-results-v1.0.0` and DOI-2 before external result communication.
