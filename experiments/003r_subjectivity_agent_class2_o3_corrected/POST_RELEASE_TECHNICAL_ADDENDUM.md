# Experiment 003R Post-Release Technical Addendum

Status: POST-RELEASE TECHNICAL CORRECTION

## 1. Purpose

This addendum records a post-release design audit of Experiment 003R. It does
not alter the preregistration, executable, generated outputs, pair-level
measurements, or registered decision produced by the frozen runner. It revises
the permissible interpretation of those measurements in light of two newly
identified design limitations.

## 2. Descriptor periodicity and effective state diversity

Experiment 003R assigned 128 administrative pair identifiers, `P2000` through
`P2127`. The registered subject descriptor is generated by affine index maps
into two lists of length 16. The complete descriptor therefore repeats every
16 indices.

The published exact-state receipts contain:

- 128 administrative pair identifiers;
- 16 unique complete A states;
- 16 unique complete B states;
- 16 unique complete A/B state pairs.

Each complete A/B state pair is repeated eight times. The two reported
history families reuse the same set of 16 complete A/B states.

Post-release reconstruction of the 16 descriptor-cycle variants found that
the `state_packet()` projection actually supplied to the Phase B carrier
produced one unique four-encounter A/B packet sequence. The complete runtime
receipts retained prompt-history differences, but those differences were not
present in the connector packet used by the O3 joint generator.

The 128 administrative identifiers must therefore not be interpreted as 128
independent content-distinct relational inputs.

## 3. Pair-exchange and history-family confounding

The registered donor index is:

`(recipient_index + 65) mod 128`.

This rule changes both donor pair and history family in 126 of 128 exchanges.
Only two completed-C exchanges remain within the recipient history family:

- `P2063` in family 0;
- `P2127` in family 1.

These are exactly the two pairs for which the published Phase A and Phase B
pair-specificity gates failed. All 126 cross-family exchanges passed.

Consequently, the observed exchange effect does not isolate pair identity from
history-family identity. The registered runner faithfully executed its frozen
donor rule, but the design does not establish that the effect was caused by
pair-specific C rather than by the simultaneously changed history family.

## 4. Impact on the published interpretation

The published numerical outputs remain valid records of the frozen execution.
The operational construction also remains executable: the third runtime,
carrier recurrence, self-state recurrence, action readout, and bilateral
return paths were run as recorded.

The following stronger interpretations are not supported by Experiment 003R
alone:

- 128 independent-pair transfer;
- content-distinct pair-indexed carrier specificity;
- a confirmatory Class 2 conclusion whose pair-specificity gate is independent
  of history-family change.

Because operational Class 2 is defined as a conjunction that includes pair
specificity, the pair-indexed Class 2 inference should be treated as
unresolved pending a corrected, independently preregistered experiment.

The result may still be cited as an exact implementation receipt for the
registered recurrent construction and as evidence that the original decision
procedure was insufficiently discriminating. It should not be cited as a
completed confirmation of content-specific relational-carrier transfer.

## 5. Reproduction scope

The public script `audit_post_release_design.py` recomputes from the published
receipts:

- the 128 administrative identifiers and 16 unique A, B, and A/B state-hash
  counts;
- the 126 cross-family and two same-family donor assignments generated by the
  frozen offset rule;
- the exact correspondence between the two same-family assignments and the
  two pair-specificity failures under the registered threshold.

The one-sequence connector-packet result was reconstructed with the
hash-frozen private subjectivity-agent runtime. Because that source is not
public, the public-only audit explicitly marks this item as not recomputed.
The claim must remain separated from the receipt-only findings unless an
independent verifier has access to the registered private source hashes.

## 6. Required correction

A corrected follow-up must:

1. generate content-unique complete A/B interaction packets;
2. verify packet-sequence uniqueness before analysis;
3. exchange completed C only between different pairs within the same history
   family;
4. keep pair exchange separate from history-order intervention;
5. treat pair, rather than seed or administrative identifier, as the primary
   inferential unit;
6. test whether C preserves A/B difference while common content is fixed;
7. distinguish early relational history from the final current input;
8. compare the proposed O3 construction with symmetric, role-aware,
   memoryless, and recurrent alternatives.

## 7. Public-record policy

This addendum is published without replacing or deleting the original E003R
preregistration or results Release. The original record, this limitation, and
the corrected follow-up remain linked as a single auditable sequence.
